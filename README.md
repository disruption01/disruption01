# Disruption01

World of Warcraft addon developer focused on bringing useful addons and modern functionality to legacy and Classic clients.

I develop, maintain, port, and backport addons for multiple World of Warcraft versions, including:

- World of Warcraft 3.3.5a / build 12340
- World of Warcraft Classic
- Classic Anniversary
- The Burning Crusade Classic
- Wrath of the Lich King Classic
- Other Classic clients when applicable

## Addon Development

My projects include:

- Modern addon backports for legacy WoW clients
- Classic ports and compatibility forks
- Maintained forks of existing addons
- Original addons and tools
- Compatibility fixes for older WoW APIs and UI systems
- Quality-of-life improvements and additional features where appropriate

For derived projects, original authors and upstream projects are always credited.

Disruption01-specific compatibility work, ports, backports, and additional features are documented separately from upstream functionality.

## Project Philosophy

My goal is to keep useful World of Warcraft addons available across older and current Classic clients while respecting the work of their original developers.

For ports, backports, and maintained forks:

- Original authorship is preserved
- Upstream projects are credited
- Original licenses are respected
- Target-client compatibility is documented
- Disruption01 additions are identified separately
- Known limitations are documented rather than hidden

## Releases

Public addon releases are distributed through their respective GitHub repositories.
